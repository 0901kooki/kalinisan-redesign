About

Filipino made, since 2004

Kalinisan manufactures sanitation chemicals in Rizal, Cebu, Davao and Cagayan de Oro, for food, beverage and institutional plants across the country.

Three manufacturing facilities, five offices, and part of the Kersia group.

Why we do this

Mission01

To provide the right sanitation solution anywhere it is needed.

With continuous improvement in our products and processes, we will provide innovative ways to answer our consumers' needs from different industries, while focusing on consumer and environmental well-being.
