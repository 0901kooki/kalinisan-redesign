Handilyn 5X

Concentrated hand soap

Up to5X

Made for food handling plants, restaurants, kitchens, supermarkets and cafeterias, where hands get washed all day.

What it is

A concentrated hand soap. Its patent-pending GPMD technology lets the concentrate keep, and even improve, its viscosity after dilution with water.

Why plants use it
  • Dilutes up to 5X for cost-efficient hand hygiene
  • Uses less plastic, storage space and water
  • pH-balanced, with a broad-spectrum bacteriostat
  • Mild enough for repeated use through the day
