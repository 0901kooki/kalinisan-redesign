Why plants use it
  • Dilutes up to 5X for cost-efficient hand hygiene
  • Patent-pending GPMD technology keeps, and even improves, viscosity after dilution
  • Uses less plastic, storage space and water
  • pH-balanced, with a broad-spectrum bacteriostat that reduces harmful bacteria on hands
  • Rinses easily with water, mild enough for repeated use through the day
